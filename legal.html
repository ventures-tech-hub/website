1

Overview

This Refund and Cancellation Policy (“Policy”) applies to all services provided by Loktara Ventures Private Limited (“Loktara”, “we”, “us” or “our”) through our website and other communication channels. By engaging with our services, you agree to the terms of this Policy.

2

Scope

This Policy covers cancellations and refund requests related to advisory services, incubation and consulting programs, paid engagements, events or any other services offered by Loktara Ventures.

3

Service Nature

Our services are specialised, knowledge-based and customised for each client or founder. Much of the work, research and resources are allocated upon confirmation of engagement. Therefore, most of our services are non-refundable once the engagement has commenced.

4

Cancellation by You

You may request to cancel your engagement by sending a written email to gagandeep.singh@loktara.com. The cancellation will be effective from the date we acknowledge your request. Any work already performed, resources allocated or costs incurred up to the cancellation date will be payable and are non-refundable.

5

Refund Policy

Refunds, if applicable, are considered only in the following cases:

  • If we are unable to provide the agreed services due to reasons solely attributable to Loktara.
  • If the engagement is cancelled before any work has commenced.

In such cases, the refund amount will be determined on a case-by-case basis, after deducting any applicable administrative costs, platform fees or third-party expenses.

6

Cancellation by Loktara

Loktara may cancel or suspend an engagement if the agreed terms are breached, required information or payments are not provided, or the engagement can no longer be delivered as agreed. In such cases, we will inform you in writing, and any refund will be assessed as set out in the Refund Policy above.

7

Non-Refundable Items

The following are non-refundable:

  • Registration fees, application fees or expression of interest fees.
  • Fees for completed advisory sessions, workshops or events.
  • Any third-party costs (legal, financial, technical, travel, etc.) incurred on your behalf.
  • Part payments for ongoing engagements.
8

Delays and Force Majeure

We are not responsible for any delays or inability to provide services due to circumstances beyond our reasonable control, including but not limited to natural disasters, government restrictions, technical outages or other force majeure events. No refunds shall be provided in such cases.

9

Changes to Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with the revised effective date. Your continued use of our services after such changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated Policy.
