About Loktara

What is Loktara?

Loktara Ventures is a venture-building and business advisory company. We work with founders and businesses across the journey from idea and validation to product development, operations, growth and scale. We also develop and support our own ventures through a combination of technology, expertise, capital and strategic partnerships.
