About NIYOOS

NIYOOS is the enterprise governance platform for AI agents. It gives organizations one place to monitor activity, enforce policy, and keep every agent aligned with business goals.

From real-time monitoring to audit-ready logs, NIYOOS gives teams complete control over AI — without slowing the work down.

The Challenge

AI agents are powerful. Without control, they are a risk.

  • Limited visibility into agent actions and decisions.
  • Uncontrolled agents create security, risk and compliance gaps.
  • Hard to keep every agent aligned with policy and goals.
  • Lack of audit trails and accountability.
  • Teams struggle to scale AI without adding operational risk.

Our Solution

NIYOOS is a unified governance and control layer for every AI agent.

  • Real-time monitoring of every agent, action and tool.
  • Assign roles and set guardrails for teams, agents and projects.
  • Loop and anomaly detection to prevent risky behavior.
  • Complete audit trails for every agent and decision.
  • Policy enforcement across agents, tools, data and vendors.

Key Features

Govern, control and grow every AI agent from one layer.
