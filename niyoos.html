About NIYOOS

NIYOOS helps enterprises govern autonomous AI agents with confidence. It provides a unified layer of control across LLMs, tools, data and workflows—so your agents act within policy, spend responsibly, and stay aligned with business goals.

From real-time monitoring to audit-ready logs, NIYOOS gives your teams the visibility and controls they need to build and scale AI safely.

The Challenge

AI agents create value—but also introduce risk at scale.

  • Limited visibility into agent actions and decisions.
  • Uncontrolled spend across models, tools and workflows.
  • Hidden loops, risky behavior and policy violations.
  • Lack of audit trails and explainability.
  • Complex compliance and data protection requirements.

Our Solution

NIYOOS brings all AI governance and control together in one platform.

  • Real-time monitoring of every agent, action and tool.
  • Budget controls and spend limits across agents and projects.
  • Loop and anomaly detection to prevent risky behavior.
  • Complete audit trails for every action and decision.
  • Policy enforcement across agents, tools, data and models.
