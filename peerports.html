About Peerports

Peerports is a student-focused platform that connects students by their skills, interests and goals, helps them build real-world projects together, and makes campus events simple to run and easy to join.

Student collaboration, project development, college events, national events and achievement showcasing now live in one connected ecosystem.

The Problem

Great skills are everywhere.
Connections are not.

  • Finding teammates with complementary skills is hard.
  • Collaborators outside your department or college are out of reach.
  • Good ideas stall for lack of the right people.
  • Skills, projects and certificates are scattered across platforms.
  • Few chances to show practical ability.

Our Solution

Peerports brings it all together on one connected platform.

  • Find the right teammates by skills and interests.
  • Build and manage real projects with your team.
  • Discover, register and participate in college and national events.
  • Get digital certificates and track your achievements.
  • Showcase your projects, participation and skills in one profile.
