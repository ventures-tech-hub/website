FAQS

Quick Answers.

How can I pitch my startup to Loktara Ventures?

You can fill out the contact form on this page selecting "Pitch Startup" or email your pitch deck directly to gagandeep.singh@loktara.com with details on your market, traction and team.

Do you invest in early-stage or only growth-stage companies?

We work with businesses across stages, from early-stage founders with high growth potential to growth-stage companies seeking strategic capital, operational support and market expansion.

Which industries do you focus on?

We focus on high-potential sectors including consumer tech, SaaS, enterprise software, climate innovation, logistics, and digital commerce.

Can we schedule a meeting in person?

Yes, we welcome in-person meetings at our Mohali Head Office by appointment. Please submit a request via our contact form to schedule an appointment.

How long does it take to get a response?

Our team reviews every submission thoroughly and typically responds within 24 to 48 business hours.
